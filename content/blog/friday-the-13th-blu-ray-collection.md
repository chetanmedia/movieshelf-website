The best Friday the 13th Blu-ray collection is Scream Factory's 16-disc **Friday the 13th Collection Deluxe Edition** from 2020: it is the only set with all 12 films, the uncut original, Part III in 3D and new 4K scans of the first four films. The cheaper alternative is Warner's 10-disc **Complete Collection** from 2013. Neither includes 4K discs, and five of the films have since come out on 4K UHD, so most collectors pair a box set with a few targeted upgrades.

That's the short answer. Below is what each set actually contains, which films are on 4K, and how to put together a complete Jason shelf without paying twice for the same movie.

:::stats
- 12 | films in the series | 1980 to 2009
- 16 | discs in the Scream Factory set | including a Blu-ray 3D disc
- 5 | films out on 4K UHD in the US | as of November 2026
:::

<div class="tldr">

**TL;DR**

- **Want everything on Blu-ray?** Buy the Scream Factory Deluxe Edition (2020). It's Region A only.
- **On a budget or outside North America?** The 2013 Complete Collection has all 12 films and is region free, but uses older transfers.
- **Want 4K?** Only the 1980 original, Part 2, Jason Goes to Hell, Jason X and the 2009 remake are available. Buy those separately.
- **Already own singles?** Catalog them first. The gaps are usually Parts V to VIII.

## Why is there no single Friday the 13th collection on 4K?

The franchise is split between two studios. Paramount released the first eight films (1980 to 1989). New Line Cinema, now part of Warner Bros., made *Jason Goes to Hell* (1993), *Jason X*, *Freddy vs. Jason* (2003), and co-produced the 2009 remake with Paramount. You can see the split in the [franchise history on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friday_the_13th_(franchise)).

That split explains almost everything about buying these films on disc:

:::cards
### Full sets need a licensing deal
Every complete box set required both studios to license their films to one label. That has happened twice: Warner in 2013 and Scream Factory in 2020.
### 4K arrives film by film
Paramount has been releasing its films on 4K one at a time. The New Line films went to Arrow Video, which licensed them separately.
### Editions go out of print
Licensed sets have limited runs. The Scream Factory set has gone in and out of stock since 2020, so used prices swing.
### Duplicates pile up
Between singles, box sets and 4K upgrades, it's easy to own the same film three times without meaning to.
:::

:::pullquote
With Friday the 13th, the complete collection is a box set plus a handful of 4K upgrades, never one purchase.
:::

## What's in the Scream Factory Friday the 13th Collection?

Scream Factory (Shout! Factory's horror label) released the **Friday the 13th Collection Deluxe Edition** on October 13, 2020. According to the [full release details](https://dailydead.com/full-release-details-for-scream-factorys-friday-the-13th-collection-deluxe-edition-16-disc-blu-ray-set/), it includes:

- **All 12 films on 16 Blu-ray discs**: 14 film discs plus 2 discs of bonus features.
- **15 versions of the films**: the original in both its R-rated theatrical cut and the uncut version, Part III in 2D and in 3D, and *Jason Goes to Hell* in both the theatrical and uncut versions.
- **New 4K scans of the original camera negatives for Parts 1 to 4**, including both the 2D and 3D versions of Part III.
- **New 2K scans** of the interpositives for *Jason Goes to Hell* and *Jason X*.
- **A separate case for each film** with the original theatrical artwork, inside a rigid slipbox with new art, plus a collectible booklet.

:::warning Region A only
The discs are locked to Region A. They play on any North American player, but not on most UK or European players. If you're outside Region A, see our guide to [Blu-ray region codes](/blog/what-blu-ray-region-is-the-us/) before you buy.
:::

The individual cases are a big deal for collectors who shelve by franchise. You can file each film in order instead of storing one large box, and the slipbox can sit on a display shelf.

## Scream Factory set vs the 2013 Complete Collection: which should you buy?

Warner Bros. released **Friday the 13th: The Complete Collection** on September 13, 2013, at a list price of $129.95. It was the first time all 12 films were sold together on Blu-ray, and seven of them made their Blu-ray debut in it, according to the [Warner press release](https://www.hometheaterforum.com/community/threads/whv-press-release-friday-the-13th-the-complete-collection-blu-ray.324334/).

:::versus Scream Factory Deluxe (2020) | Complete Collection (2013)
- 16 Blu-ray discs, each film in its own case
- 15 versions, including the uncut original and uncut *Jason Goes to Hell*
- New 4K scans of Parts 1 to 4
- Two discs of new and archival extras
- Usually the more expensive of the two
---
- 9 Blu-ray discs plus a DVD of extras, in a tin with a book-style case
- Region free
- Older transfers carried over from earlier releases
- Part III in anaglyph (red/blue glasses) 3D
- Often cheaper on the used market
:::

Here's how they compare on the details that matter most:

| | Scream Factory Deluxe (2020) | Complete Collection (2013) |
|---|---|---|
| Films | All 12 | All 12 |
| Discs | 16 Blu-ray | 9 Blu-ray + 1 DVD |
| Part III in 3D | Blu-ray 3D | Anaglyph (red/blue) |
| Uncut *Jason Goes to Hell* | Yes | No |
| New scans | Parts 1–4 in 4K, IX–X in 2K | No, older transfers |
| Packaging | Individual cases in a slipbox | Book-style case in a tin |
| Region | A (locked) | Region free |

**Buy the Scream Factory set** if you want one definitive Blu-ray shelf, care about the uncut versions, or plan to keep it for years. **Buy the 2013 set** if you just want to watch all 12 films, live outside Region A, or find it for much less.

One thing to test on a used 2013 set: [High-Def Digest's review](https://bluray.highdefdigest.com/9439/friday_13th_complete.html) reported that *The Final Chapter* disc froze and skipped around the 59-minute mark on all three players it tried.

:::tip Check the bonus features before you sell your old discs
Some older singles have extras that didn't make it into the later sets. Before you sell or donate them, compare the special features lists on a review site such as [Blu-ray.com](https://www.blu-ray.com/movies/Friday-the-13th-Collection-Blu-ray/272680/).
:::

## Which Friday the 13th movies are on 4K UHD?

As of November 2026, five of the 12 films have a US 4K release. Each came out separately:

| Film | Year | 4K UHD release (US) |
|---|---|---|
| Friday the 13th | 1980 | Paramount, September 2022 (theatrical and unrated cuts) |
| Friday the 13th Part 2 | 1981 | Paramount Scares Vol. 2 box set (October 2024), standalone (February 2026) |
| Part III to Part VIII | 1982–1989 | None yet: Blu-ray only |
| Jason Goes to Hell: The Final Friday | 1993 | Arrow Video limited edition, May 2025 (theatrical and unrated cuts) |
| Jason X | 2001 | Arrow Video limited edition, May 2025 |
| Freddy vs. Jason | 2003 | None yet: Blu-ray only |
| Friday the 13th (remake) | 2009 | Arrow Video limited edition, September 2024 (theatrical cut and Killer Cut) |

A few details to know before buying:

- **Part 2 is the same disc twice.** The 2026 standalone release uses the same 4K disc as the Paramount Scares Vol. 2 set, which also includes *Orphan: First Kill*, *World War Z* and *Breakdown*. Only the packaging differs.
- **Arrow's 2009 remake has no 1080p disc.** Both cuts are on 4K discs, so you need a 4K player to watch it.
- **Arrow limited editions sell out.** Once the first pressing goes, Arrow sometimes reissues a standard edition with less packaging. Check [Arrow Video's site](https://arrowvideo.com/4k/jason-goes-to-hell-limited-edition-4k-uhd/16247822.html) for what's in stock.

Is the upgrade worth it? The Paramount discs for the original and Part 2 both carry Dolby Vision and HDR10, which a Blu-ray can't. If you're unsure whether your setup will show the difference, our [4K vs Blu-ray guide](/blog/4k-blu-ray-vs-blu-ray/) explains what you actually gain.

## How do you build a complete Friday the 13th collection without buying twice?

Most fans end up with a mixed shelf: a box set for the films that are only on Blu-ray and 4K discs for the rest. Here's an order that avoids wasted money:

:::steps
### Catalog what you already own
Check for singles, old DVDs and steelbooks before you buy anything. Many collectors already own Parts 1 to 3 as Paramount singles or steelbooks, or the original on 4K.
### Pick your Blu-ray base
Choose the Scream Factory set for completeness or the 2013 set for price. Either one covers Parts III to VIII and *Freddy vs. Jason*, which aren't on 4K.
### Add the 4K upgrades you care about
The 1980 original and Part 2 from Paramount, and the three New Line films from Arrow. Skip the ones you rarely watch.
### Decide what to do with the duplicates
Keep the box set intact if you might sell it later. A complete set is usually worth more than the sum of its loose discs.
### Record the edition, not just the film
Note "Arrow 4K, Killer Cut" or "Scream Factory, uncut" so you know exactly which version you own.
:::

## What should you check before buying a used set?

Both box sets are now mostly bought used or from marketplace sellers, and missing discs are the most common problem. Go through this list before you pay:

:::checklist Used box set checklist
- All **16 discs** (Scream Factory) or **10 discs** (2013 set) are present, including the bonus discs
- The **Blu-ray 3D disc** of Part III (Scream Factory) or the **3D glasses** (2013 set) are included
- Discs are free of deep scratches and **disc rot** (look for speckles held up to a light)
- The **booklet and slipbox** are included, if you care about resale value
- The listing photos show the actual set, not a stock image
- The Scream Factory set's **Region A** lock works with your player
:::

Disc rot is easy to miss in listing photos, so ask for close-ups. Our [disc rot guide](/blog/disc-rot/) shows what to look for.
