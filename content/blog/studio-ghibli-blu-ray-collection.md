The best way to build a Studio Ghibli Blu-ray collection in the US is to buy the GKIDS releases film by film: every Ghibli feature is available as a Blu-ray + DVD combo pack, usually for under $20, with both the Japanese audio and the English dub. Upgrade to a steelbook or collector's edition only for the films you love most. There is no official complete box set, and only one Ghibli film, *The Boy and the Heron*, is on 4K UHD in the US.

That's the short answer. Below is what each edition actually gets you, where 4K stands, and how to fill the shelf without buying a bootleg or the same film twice.

:::stats
- 2017 | GKIDS takes over US Ghibli discs | from Disney
- 1 | Ghibli film on 4K UHD in the US | The Boy and the Heron
- 0 | official complete box sets | anything "complete" is a bootleg
:::

- **Starting out?** Buy the standard GKIDS Blu-ray + DVD of each film. List price is $29.95, but the GKIDS store often sells them for $16.99.
- **Want them on display?** GKIDS steelbooks list at $39.98 and are reissued in waves, so don't panic-buy on eBay.
- **Want 4K?** Only *The Boy and the Heron* in the US. *Princess Mononoke* is on 4K in Japan and France, with a US release listed but undated.

## Who releases Studio Ghibli Blu-rays in the US?

[GKIDS](https://store.gkids.com/collections/studio-ghibli-collection), with Shout! Factory handling distribution. Disney held Ghibli's US home video rights for two decades, then in 2017 GKIDS took over and reissued the whole catalog, including films Disney never put on Blu-ray, like *Only Yesterday* and *Ocean Waves*.

That history matters when you shop used:

:::cards
### Disney discs are out of print
You'll still see Disney Blu-rays of *Spirited Away* and *Totoro* in used bins. They're fine discs, but the GKIDS versions usually match or beat them.
### GKIDS encodes are often better
Blu-ray.com's review of the GKIDS [*Princess Mononoke* Blu-ray](https://www.blu-ray.com/movies/Princess-Mononoke-Blu-ray/184140/) measured a 29 Mbps video bitrate, up from about 22 Mbps on the old Disney disc.
### Every film is a combo pack
GKIDS sells each film as Blu-ray + DVD in one case. That's handy for a kids' room or a car player.
### Both languages included
The original Japanese audio with English subtitles and the English dub are on the same disc, so nobody in the house has to compromise.
:::

:::pullquote
For Ghibli, the edition question is about the box, not the movie: the standard disc is already the good one.
:::

## Which Ghibli edition should you buy: standard, steelbook or collector's?

GKIDS has released three tiers. The film disc inside is the core of all of them; what changes is the packaging and extras.

| Edition | What you get | Typical price | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard Blu-ray + DVD | Film on Blu-ray and DVD, both audio tracks, extras like storyboards and trailers | $29.95 list, often $16.99 at GKIDS | Building the full set cheaply |
| Limited Edition Steelbook | Metal case with exclusive art, Blu-ray + DVD | $39.98 list, often $33.99 | Display shelves, favorite films |
| Collector's Edition | Oversized box, art book, soundtrack CD, Blu-ray | Varies, mostly second-hand now | Superfans of *Totoro*, *Spirited Away* or *Mononoke* |
| 4K UHD + Blu-ray | 2160p disc with Dolby Vision and Atmos | $39.98 list | *The Boy and the Heron* only, for now |

The Collector's Editions are the real prizes. The [*Spirited Away* set from 2019](https://animationmagazine.net/2019/08/studio-ghiblis-spirited-away-gets-collector-set-treatment), for example, came in a hard slipcase with a disc portfolio, the soundtrack on CD and a 40-page art book with essays by Kenneth Turan and Leonard Maltin. *My Neighbor Totoro* and *Princess Mononoke* got the same treatment.

:::bars US list price per edition (GKIDS store)
- Standard Blu-ray + DVD | 29.95 | $29.95
- Steelbook (Blu-ray + DVD) | 39.98 | $39.98
- The Boy and the Heron 4K + Blu-ray | 39.98 | $39.98
- The Boy and the Heron 4K steelbook | 44.98 | $44.98
:::

:::tip Steelbooks come back
GKIDS has reissued many of its Ghibli steelbooks. *Spirited Away*, *Totoro*, *Kiki's Delivery Service* and *Grave of the Fireflies* are all sold as "Steelbook Reissue" in the store. If one sells out, wait before paying a reseller double. Our guide to [what a steelbook is](/blog/what-is-a-steelbook/) covers whether the metal case is worth it.
:::

## Are Studio Ghibli movies on 4K Blu-ray?

Barely, so far. Here's where things stand in November 2026:

- ***The Boy and the Heron*** was [Ghibli's first 4K UHD release](https://gkids.com/2024/05/25/the-boy-and-the-heron-to-be-released-on-4k-uhd-blu-ray-blu-ray-dvd-and-a-limited-edition-steelbook-july-9-2024/), out from GKIDS on July 9, 2024, with Dolby Vision HDR and Dolby Atmos, as a standard 4K + Blu-ray and a 4K steelbook.
- ***Princess Mononoke*** got a new 4K restoration that played in IMAX in 2025. Japan released it on 4K UHD on June 24, 2026, with Dolby Vision and a region-free 4K disc. A French 4K steelbook from Wild Bunch is due December 11, 2026. A US 4K disc has been listed but had no release date when we wrote this.
- ***Kiki's Delivery Service*** has a new 4K restoration that GKIDS screened in IMAX theaters in 2026. No disc has been announced.

:::warning Read the import small print
On the Japanese *Princess Mononoke* 4K, the English subtitles and English dub are on the **Blu-ray disc only**, not the 4K disc. The French edition has French subtitles only. If you want 4K with English, wait for the US release.
:::

If you're wondering whether the jump to 4K is worth it for hand-drawn animation, our [4K vs Blu-ray guide](/blog/4k-blu-ray-vs-blu-ray/) explains what HDR and the extra resolution actually change.

## Is there a complete Studio Ghibli Blu-ray box set?

No. There has never been an official set with every Ghibli film, so any listing promising "every film" or "the complete Studio Ghibli collection" in one box is a bootleg. Fans on r/Miyazaki keep a pinned FAQ about exactly this, because these sets show up constantly on eBay and Amazon marketplaces.

The legitimate box sets are narrower:

- **The Collected Works of Hayao Miyazaki** (Blu-ray, 2015): an [Amazon exclusive from Disney](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2015-07-30/disney-to-release-collected-works-of-hayao-miyazaki-blu-ray-set/.91094) with Miyazaki's 11 features up to *The Wind Rises*, a bonus disc and a book. It's long out of print and sells second-hand for well above its launch price.
- **The Collected Works of Isao Takahata**, released in Japan, collecting the other Ghibli co-founder's films.

:::versus Genuine GKIDS disc | Likely bootleg
- GKIDS and Shout! Factory logos on the case
- Blu-ray + DVD combo, one film per case
- Sold by GKIDS, major retailers or a known seller
---
- "Complete collection" of every film in one box
- Price far too low for the number of films
- Odd English on the box, no studio logos, ships from overseas
:::

## Which Ghibli films should you start with?

If you're building a collection from zero, start with the films people rewatch most, then fill in the quieter ones. These six cover the range of what Ghibli does:

:::cards
### Spirited Away (2001)
Miyazaki's Oscar winner, and the one most worth a steelbook or Collector's Edition.
### My Neighbor Totoro (1988)
The family rewatch. The DVD in the combo pack is perfect for a kids' room.
### Princess Mononoke (1997)
The one to watch for a US 4K release. Buy the Blu-ray now if you can't wait.
### Kiki's Delivery Service (1989)
Freshly restored in 4K for theaters, so a disc upgrade may follow.
### Howl's Moving Castle (2004)
A steelbook favorite with collectors, and the easiest Miyazaki film to love on a first watch.
### The Tale of the Princess Kaguya (2013)
Isao Takahata's watercolor masterpiece, and the best argument for owning the Takahata films too.
:::

Then add the Takahata and non-Miyazaki films that casual fans tend to miss: *Only Yesterday*, *Pom Poko*, *Whisper of the Heart*, *When Marnie Was There* and *Ocean Waves*. These are often the cheapest discs in the GKIDS store.

## How do you organize a Studio Ghibli shelf?

Most collectors shelve Ghibli as its own section rather than alphabetically with everything else. Two orders work well: **release order**, which tells the studio's story from *Castle in the Sky* (1986) onward, or **by director**, which groups Miyazaki, Takahata and the younger directors.

## How do you track a Ghibli collection without buying doubles?

Ghibli is where duplicates happen. The same film exists as a Disney Blu-ray, a GKIDS combo pack, a steelbook, a reissued steelbook and sometimes a Collector's Edition, and they all look similar in a store. A simple routine keeps it straight:

:::steps
### Scan every Ghibli disc you already own
The barcode identifies the exact release, so a GKIDS steelbook and a GKIDS standard edition become two different entries.
### Record the edition, not just the film
Note "Steelbook reissue" or "Collector's Edition" and whether it's the Disney or GKIDS release.
### Put the Ghibli section in its own folder
That makes gaps easy to see: count what's there against the films you want.
### Keep a wishlist of specific editions
"Princess Mononoke, US 4K" is a far more useful note than "Mononoke."
:::

## Start with the films you rewatch

You don't need every edition to have a great Ghibli shelf. Buy the standard GKIDS discs of the films your household watches most, save steelbook money for one or two favorites, and keep a list of what you own so the next sale doesn't trick you into a second *Totoro*.
