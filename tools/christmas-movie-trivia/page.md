## How to play

:::steps
### Pick your pace
Play against the clock for speed bonuses, or choose Relaxed and take your time.
### Answer ten questions
Tap an answer, or press A, B, C or D on a keyboard. You'll see the right answer straight away.
### Build a streak
Three right in a row gives you 1.5 times the points. Five in a row gives you double.
### Beat your best
Your best score is saved in your browser. Share your rank and challenge your friends.
:::
