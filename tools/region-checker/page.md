## How to read the region code on a disc

The region is printed on the back of the case, usually near the runtime and audio details. DVDs show a small globe with a number. Blu-rays show a letter, A, B or C, in a rounded badge.

:::cards
### A number in a globe
A DVD region, 1 to 6. A globe with several numbers, or the word ALL or 0, means the disc plays in each of them.
### A letter in a badge
A Blu-ray region. A, B and C together, or no badge at all, usually means the disc is region-free.
### No badge on a 4K disc
Normal. The 4K UHD Blu-ray format has no region coding, so there's nothing to print.
:::

:::tip Check every disc in the box
A 4K combo pack can hold a region-free 4K disc, a Region B Blu-ray and a Region 2 DVD. Each one follows its own rules.
:::

## DVD and Blu-ray regions compared

DVD and Blu-ray use two separate systems, and they don't line up. Japan is the best-known surprise: it shares DVD Region 2 with Europe but Blu-ray Region A with the US.

| Area | DVD region | Blu-ray region |
|---|---|---|
| United States, Canada | 1 | A |
| Mexico, Central and South America | 4 | A |
| UK and most of Europe | 2 | B |
| Japan | 2 | A |
| South Korea, Taiwan, Hong Kong | 3 | A |
| Southeast Asia | 3 | A |
| Australia, New Zealand | 4 | B |
| Middle East, Egypt, South Africa | 2 | B |
| Most of Africa | 5 | B |
| India, Pakistan, Bangladesh | 5 | C |
| Russia, Ukraine, Belarus | 5 | C |
| Mainland China | 6 | C |
| 4K UHD Blu-ray | No regions | No regions |

The map in the checker colors every country by its region. Region boundaries come from the published region definitions summarized by [German Way](https://german-way.com/german-way-store/dvd-and-blu-ray/blu-ray-and-dvd-region-codes) and [The Home Cinema Guide](https://www.the-home-cinema-guide.com/blu-ray-regions.html). For a few places, such as the South Caucasus and some territories, sources disagree, so the checker asks you to confirm with the label on your player.

## What to do when a disc won't play

:::steps
### Look for the 4K edition
If the film is out on 4K UHD, that disc has no region lock and plays on any 4K player.
### Check for a release in your region
Many imports get a local release later. It may have different extras, so compare before you wait.
### Consider a region-free player
It plays discs from every region. Many players sold as "multi-region" only unlock DVDs, so look for "Blu-ray Region A/B/C" in the specs.
### Mind PAL and NTSC for DVDs
Most DVDs from Europe, Australia and Asia outside Japan are PAL. A US setup needs a player that converts PAL, or a TV that accepts 50Hz.
:::
