## Why Blu-ray cases are shorter than DVD cases

The disc inside is the same 120 mm size, so the difference is all packaging. Blu-ray cases were designed about 18.5 mm shorter and a little thinner than DVD cases, with a logo banner across the top, so you can tell the formats apart on a shelf at a glance. 4K UHD kept the Blu-ray footprint and switched to black plastic.

:::tip Matching a cracked case
Measure the spine of a case next to it on the shelf before you buy a replacement. A 14 mm cover wrapped around a 12 mm case leaves a soft fold, and a thicker case won't slide back into its slipcover. Our [DVD case replacement guide](/blog/dvd-case-replacement/) covers hubs, colors and where to buy.
:::

## How much shelf space do cases take?

Thickness decides how many cases fit on a shelf. A foot of shelf holds about 21 standard DVDs, 24 Blu-rays or 38 slim DVD cases. The [shelf space calculator](/tools/shelf-calculator/) works out the full length for your whole collection, and how many IKEA KALLAX cubes or BILLY shelves that is.
