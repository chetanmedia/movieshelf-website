## How to play Guess the Movie

:::steps
### Look at the emoji
Three emoji describe one film. Some are literal, some are puns.
### Make a guess
Start typing and pick a film from the list. You have six tries.
### Unlock clues as you go
Each wrong guess or skip reveals another clue. First the year and genre, then the plot explained badly, then the director, the star, and finally the shape of the title.
### Share your score
Share your result without giving the answer away, then come back tomorrow to keep your streak going.
:::

:::tip December is all Christmas films
From December 1st to Christmas Day, every film is a Christmas classic. If you're hosting a movie night, play it together before the film starts.
:::

## Make it a movie night game

Guess the Movie works well with a group. Put it on the TV, shout out guesses and see who gets it first. When you've guessed today's film, try a past day, or spin the [random movie generator](/tools/random-movie/) to pick tonight's film.
