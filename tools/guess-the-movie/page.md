## How to play Guess the Movie

:::steps
### Start with almost nothing
The first film of the day is three emoji. The other nine start with just the year and genre.
### Make a guess
Start typing and pick a film from the list. You have six tries per film.
### Unlock clues as you go
Each wrong guess or skip reveals another clue: a tagline, the cast, the director, the plot with the title blanked out, and finally the shape of the title.
### Score, share and come back
Fewer tries earn more points. Share your ten squares without giving anything away, then come back tomorrow to keep your streak going.
:::

:::tip December is all Christmas films
From December 1st to Christmas Day, the emoji film that opens each day's set is a Christmas classic. If you're hosting a movie night, play it together before the film starts.
:::

## Make it a movie night game

Guess the Movie works well with a group. Put it on the TV, shout out guesses and see who gets it first. When you've finished today's 10, switch to Endless mode, try a past day, or spin the [random movie generator](/tools/random-movie/) to pick tonight's film.
