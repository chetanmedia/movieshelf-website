## How the calculator works

The calculator multiplies the number of cases in each format by that format's spine width, adds any box sets you measured, then adds the room to grow you picked. It then divides the total by the usable width of popular shelves.

:::stats
- 21 | DVDs per foot | standard 14 mm case
- 24 | Blu-rays per foot | standard 12.5 mm case
- 26 | Blu-rays per KALLAX cube | one row, 33 cm wide
:::

These are the spine widths it uses. You can change any of them under **Adjust case sizes**, since a few studios and countries use thicker or thinner cases.

| Format | Spine width | Height | Per foot | Per meter |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DVD (standard keep case) | 14 mm | 190 mm | 21 | 71 |
| Blu-ray | 12.5 mm | 171.5 mm | 24 | 80 |
| 4K UHD | 13 mm | 171.5 mm | 23 | 76 |
| Steelbook (approx.) | 14 mm | about 176 mm | 21 | 71 |
| Slim DVD case | 8 mm | 184 mm | 38 | 125 |
| VHS (approx.) | 27 mm | about 205 mm | 11 | 37 |

Sources: DVD and slim case sizes from [Ronyasoft's case dimension guide](https://www.ronyasoft.com/products/cd-dvd-label-maker/articles/popular_dvd_case_dimensions), 4K UHD case sizes from [Duplication.com's UHD case specs](https://www.duplication.com/4k-uhd-cases). Blu-ray spines are typically 11 to 12 mm for US cases and 14 mm for UK cases, so 12.5 mm is a middle value.

## How many discs fit in IKEA shelves?

KALLAX and BILLY are the two most popular shelves for disc collections, and both fit DVDs and Blu-rays comfortably in height.

| Shelf | Usable width | DVDs | Blu-rays | 4K UHDs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| KALLAX cube (one row) | 33 cm | 23 | 26 | 25 |
| KALLAX cube (two rows deep) | 33 cm × 2 | 46 | 52 | 50 |
| BILLY 80 cm, per shelf | 76 cm | 54 | 60 | 58 |
| BILLY 40 cm, per shelf | 36 cm | 25 | 28 | 27 |

:::tip Two rows in a KALLAX
A KALLAX compartment is deep enough for two rows of cases, one behind the other. It doubles capacity, but you can't see the back row. If you go two deep, keep a catalog so you can find what's hidden.
:::

A BILLY shelf is only about 26 cm deep, so it takes a single row. BILLY's shelves are adjustable, though, and DVDs are only 19 cm tall. You can buy extra shelves and fit more levels into one bookcase than the six it comes with.

## Tips for planning disc shelves

:::steps
### Measure, don't guess
Count by format, not just a total. 200 Blu-rays need about 10% less shelf than 200 DVDs.
### Measure box sets separately
Box sets vary too much to estimate. Put a tape measure along them and enter the total width.
### Leave room to grow
A shelf packed edge to edge means reshuffling everything for each new purchase. 20% spare space is a good default.
### Group by height
DVDs are about 2 cm taller than Blu-rays. Keeping each format together gives you clean, even rows.
:::
