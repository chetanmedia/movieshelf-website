## Cover sizes for every case

These are the trim sizes the cover maker uses. Each one has 3 mm of bleed added on every edge, so the artwork still reaches the edge after cutting.

| Case | Front + spine + back | Full cover (trim) | In inches |
|---|---|---|---|
| DVD, standard 14 mm | 129.5 + 14 + 129.5 mm | 273 × 183 mm | 10.75 × 7.2 in |
| Slim DVD, 7 mm | 129.5 + 7 + 129.5 mm | 266 × 183 mm | 10.47 × 7.2 in |
| Blu-ray and 4K UHD, 12 mm | 129 + 12 + 129 mm | 270 × 148 mm | 10.63 × 5.83 in |
| Blu-ray UK, 14 mm | 129 + 14 + 129 mm | 272 × 148 mm | 10.71 × 5.83 in |
| Multi-disc Blu-ray, 15 mm | 129 + 15 + 129 mm | 273 × 148 mm | 10.75 × 5.83 in |

Sources: the standard DVD size matches [Avery's DVD case inserts](https://www.avery.co.uk/product/dvd-case-inserts-j8437-25) and [4over4's DVD cover specs](https://www.4over4.com/guide/dvd-cover-size-dimensions-and-print-specs). Blu-ray panel and spine widths come from [Duplication.com's Blu-ray case listings](https://duplication.com/Blu-ray-Cases), and heights from [Ronyasoft's Blu-ray cover guide](https://www.ronyasoft.com/products/cd-dvd-label-maker/tutorials/how_to_make_a_blu_ray_cover/).

:::warning Measure your case first
Spines vary by a millimetre or two between brands and countries. Lay a ruler across the spine of your actual case and type it into the cover maker. Everything else adjusts automatically.
:::

## How to print and fit your cover

:::steps
### Pick the right download
Use **PDF on Letter or A4 with crop marks** for a home printer. Use **Download PDF** (exact size with bleed) if a print shop is printing it.
### Print at 100% scale
In the print dialog, turn off "fit to page" or "shrink to fit". Otherwise the cover comes out slightly too small for the sleeve.
### Cut along the crop marks
The solid marks show the trim edge. A paper trimmer or a craft knife and ruler gives the cleanest edge.
### Fold at the spine marks
The dashed marks at the top and bottom show the two spine folds. Score lightly with a blunt edge before folding.
### Slide it into the case sleeve
Start with the spine, then tuck in the front and back.
:::

## Tips for a cover that looks professional

:::cards
### Use a big enough image
For a sharp print, the front image should be at least 1,500 pixels wide. Smaller images look soft once printed.
### Keep text inside the safe area
The dotted blue box in the preview is 3 mm inside the edge. Anything outside it risks being trimmed off.
### Match your spines
Collectors often give a shelf of custom covers the same spine font, color and numbering, so a run of films lines up neatly.
### Pick the reading direction
US spines read top to bottom. UK and European spines read bottom to top. Use the one that matches the rest of your shelf.
:::
