## How to use the random movie generator

:::steps
### Pick where to spin from
Popular films covers the films most people have heard of. Hidden gems digs up highly rated films with a smaller following. Christmas is for the season, and My own list spins only films you choose.
### Narrow it down if you like
Filter by genre, decade, length and rating. Pick two genres, such as Comedy and Romance, to get films that are both.
### Spin
The reel lands on one film, with its poster, runtime, rating, director, cast and a short plot. Not feeling it? Spin again.
### Save it or skip it
Save a pick for later with the heart, or mark it as seen so it never comes up again.
:::

:::tip Movie night with a group
Let everyone add a film or two in My own list, then spin. Nobody can complain about the choice, because nobody made it.
:::

## Spin your own shelf

If you collect DVDs, Blu-rays or 4K discs, the best list to spin is the one on your shelf. You already own those films, so there's nothing to rent or stream. Paste your titles into My own list and let the reel decide.
